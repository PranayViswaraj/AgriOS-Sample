Field Sample AG-1024
Targeted Foliar Disease ETL Exceeded

Rice Leaf Blast (Magnaporthe oryzae)

Typical spindle-shaped foliar necrotic lesions with ash-grey centers and distinct dark brown margins. Economic Threshold Level (ETL) breached with 14.2% leaf canopy involvement across surveyed tiller clusters.

Vulnerability Phase: Maximum Tillering • Fungal Spore Index: 8.9/10
1

Cultural Control & Moisture Modulation

Immediate Action
  • Canopy Aeration: Drain standing water immediately from affected plots to lower micro-canopy relative humidity below 85%.
  • Fertilizer Moratorium: Strictly suspend top-dressing of Urea and all nitrogenous chemical fertilizers for 10 days; excess free nitrogen accelerates blast mycelial proliferation.
  • Water Regimen: Maintain alternate wetting and moderate drying (AWD) regime after initial field drainage until new tillers show healthy vigor.
2

Biological Control (Bio-Antagonist Application)

TNAU Pf1 Form

Apply foliar prophylactic spray of Pseudomonas fluorescens (TNAU Strain Pf1) @ 2.5 kg/ha (or 5g per litre of water). Mix with 100ml bio-surfactant or liquid khadi soap per spray tank for optimum foliar adhesion during early vegetative transition.

eco Available at local Kumbakonam PACCS & Agri Extension Depots @ 50% state subsidy.
